⚠ IMPORTANT GUIDELINES

💊
MEDICINES
  • Always check the expiry date before adding a medicine.
  • Do not donate expired or damaged medicines.
  • Mention the correct quantity and medicine type.
  • Keep medicines in their original packaging whenever possible.
♿
MEDICAL EQUIPMENT
  • Add only equipment that is safe and usable.
  • Clearly mention the condition of the equipment.
  • Do not list damaged or unsafe equipment.
  • Upload clear images so the receiver can verify its condition.
📋
INFORMATION
  • Enter correct contact details so needy people/NGOs can reach you.
  • Do not upload unnecessary personal or confidential information.
  • Verify all details before submitting.
⚠
IMPORTANT

MedAid connects donors with people and NGOs in need.

Please verify the condition, expiry, and suitability of any medicine or equipment before donating or accepting it.
